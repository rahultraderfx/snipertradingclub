Forex in a minute

Forex (foreign exchange) is the market where currencies are traded in pairs, like EURUSD or USDJPY. Buy one currency and you sell the other, so you can aim to profit whether a currency rises or falls. Gold (XAUUSD) trades on the same platforms.

What makes it special

• Open around the clock from Monday to Friday, passing from Sydney to Tokyo, London and New York.
• The biggest financial market on Earth, so major pairs are easy to buy and sell.
• You can trade both directions: buy when you expect a rise, sell when you expect a fall.
• Small sizes (0.01 lot) let you start small and learn.
• Leverage magnifies profits and losses. That is why risk management comes first.

Moments that moved the market

1971The dollar leaves gold

The US ends the dollar’s link to gold. Fixed exchange rates collapse and currencies start to float, creating the modern forex market.

16 Sep 1992Black Wednesday

Sterling is forced out of Europe’s exchange rate system after huge selling. Famous traders profit from the fall.

15 Jan 2015Swiss franc shock

The Swiss National Bank drops its cap on the franc. The franc surges within minutes and many traders and brokers take heavy losses.

24 Jun 2016Brexit vote

Sterling plunges to about a 30-year low against the dollar overnight.

26 Sep 2022UK mini-budget panic

Sterling falls to a record low near 1.03 against the dollar.

Lesson: big news can move prices in seconds. Always use a stop loss and size your risk.
